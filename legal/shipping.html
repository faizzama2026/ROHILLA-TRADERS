Shipping Policy

Last updated:

Where we ship

We ship across India through reputed courier partners (Shiprocket, Delhivery, India Post, Bluedart). Check serviceability at checkout by entering your pincode.

Charges

  • Flat ₹49 on orders below ₹500
  • FREE shipping on orders of ₹500 or more
  • COD (Cash on Delivery) is available on most pincodes (additional ₹30 COD handling fee may apply)

Processing time

Orders are typically dispatched within 1–2 working days of confirmation. You will receive a tracking number on WhatsApp / email as soon as your parcel is handed over to the courier.

Estimated delivery times

  • Bareilly & nearby: 1 day
  • Other UP & Delhi NCR: 2–3 days
  • Tier 1 metros (Mumbai / Bangalore / Chennai / Hyderabad / Kolkata): 4–6 days
  • Tier 2 / 3 towns & remote pincodes: 5–8 days

These are estimates. Festive seasons, weather and regional disruptions can add 2–3 days.

Tracking

You can track your order with the tracking number we send you. If you don't receive an update within 3 working days of placing your order, please contact us.

Failed delivery / address issues

If the courier is unable to deliver after 3 attempts due to incorrect address or non-availability, the parcel will be returned to us. Re-shipping will require fresh shipping charges. Refunds for returned-to-origin parcels will be processed minus original shipping cost.

Damaged in transit

If your order arrives damaged, please open the parcel in front of the delivery agent and report damage immediately. Take a photo of the damaged outer packaging and contact us within 24 hours of delivery. We will replace damaged items at no cost.

International shipping

We currently ship only within India. International shipping is on our roadmap — please email us if you'd like to be notified.
