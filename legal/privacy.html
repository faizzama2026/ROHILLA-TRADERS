Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

1. Who we are

This privacy policy explains how M/S Rohilla Traders ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses and protects information about you when you visit our website or place an order.

2. What information we collect

We collect only the information needed to fulfil your order and respond to your queries:

  • Identity & Contact: name, phone number, email address
  • Delivery details: shipping address, pincode, city, state
  • Order details: products, quantities, prices, order notes
  • Payment information: once online payment is enabled, it is handled directly by our payment partner (Razorpay). We do not see or store your card / UPI details.
  • Communications: messages you send us on WhatsApp / email

3. How we use it

  • To process and deliver your order
  • To send order confirmations and shipping updates
  • To respond to customer support enquiries
  • To comply with Indian tax and consumer-protection law
  • If you opt in on WhatsApp: to send occasional recipes and offers (you can opt out any time)

4. Who we share it with

We share the minimum necessary data with:

  • Courier partners (e.g. Shiprocket, Delhivery, India Post) to deliver your order
  • Razorpay, for processing payments — covered by their own privacy policy
  • Government authorities, only if required by law

We do not sell, rent or trade your information to anyone for marketing.

5. How long we keep it

Order and tax records are retained for 8 years to comply with the Income Tax Act and GST law. Marketing-list subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. Email — with the subject "Data Request" and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies

We use a single browser localStorage entry to remember the items in your cart. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Security

The website is served over HTTPS. Payment data is processed only by Razorpay, a PCI-DSS compliant gateway. We never request or store your card / UPI PIN / OTP.

9. Children

Our products are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect changes.
