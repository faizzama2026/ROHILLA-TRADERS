Refund & Returns Policy

Last updated:

Because we sell consumable food products, our return policy is shaped by food-safety law. Once a sealed pouch leaves our warehouse, we cannot resell it. So returns are limited to the situations below — but in every one of these, we will make it right.

When you CAN return / get a refund

  1. Damaged in transit: Pouch is torn, leaking, or the outer box is visibly damaged on arrival. Report within 24 hours of delivery with photos.
  2. Wrong product shipped: You ordered Kebab Masala and received Tandoori Masala. Report within 48 hours of delivery.
  3. Quality complaint: Foreign matter, off odour, infestation, or significantly off-flavour. Report within 7 days of delivery. We may ask you to send the unused product back at our cost for inspection.
  4. Missing items: Your order is short by one or more pouches. Report within 48 hours.
  5. Order cancelled before dispatch: Full refund within 5 working days.

When we CANNOT accept returns

  • Opened pouches (food-safety law)
  • "Don't like the taste" — taste is subjective and our pouches list every ingredient. Please read carefully before ordering. As a goodwill gesture, first-time customers may request a partial refund on one product per order within 7 days; this is at our sole discretion.
  • Reports made after the timeframes listed above

How to raise a return / refund request

  1. WhatsApp — or email — with your order number and a clear photo of the issue.
  2. We respond within 24 working hours.
  3. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–7 working days, or as a UPI transfer for COD orders.

Cancellations

You can cancel an order any time before we dispatch it (usually within 24 hours of placing) for a full refund. Once the order is dispatched, the shipping policy applies.

Refund timelines

  • UPI / Wallet: 1–3 working days
  • Credit / Debit card: 5–7 working days (depending on issuing bank)
  • Net banking: 3–5 working days
  • COD: UPI transfer within 5 working days of approval
